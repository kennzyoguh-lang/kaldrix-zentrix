Try it yourself

Ten minutes, with an AI assistant you already use.

  1. Make a test account at the test service. Accept the testing agreement and keep the key it shows once. Add a supplier.
  2. In the dashboard, make a key for your AI assistant. That key can only pay and read payments. It cannot create suppliers, permissions or approvals.
  3. Install the connector in Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Codex or any MCP assistant. Instructions are here.
  4. Give the agent a permission: this supplier, this much, until this time. Ask it to pay. Ask it to pay too much. Read the reason it is refused.
  5. A large payment waits in the dashboard. Approve it as the person, then watch it go through. Cancel the permission and try again.
  6. Want to try database access? Give the agent a data permission for the demo database (a support desk with a payroll table it must not touch), for one minute, and watch it read tickets and be refused everything else. See how it works.

Questions design partners ask first

Does it cost anything to be a design partner?

No. Design partnership is free and runs in test mode with a pretend bank. A paid pilot, if you want one later, is a separate written agreement.

Does real money move?

No. Nothing real moves during the design-partner stage. Real money needs an independent security review and a signed agreement with a live payment provider first.

Is the letter of intent binding?

No. The letter is non-binding except for confidentiality, no exclusivity and no use of names without permission. It records that we will work together in good faith for 90 days.

What do you do with my data and my name?

Test data stays in the test service. We will not publish your name, logo or results without your written permission. Your feedback may be used to improve the product.

What do you need from us?

About three hours of someone's time over a month: one introduction call, one session connecting an agent, and one review call. A named person who can say what a safe payment limit looks like for your workflow.

Can we stop at any time?

Yes. Either side can end the programme at any time by writing to the other.
