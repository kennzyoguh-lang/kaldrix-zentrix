MCP payment tools: how to keep them safe

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) lets an AI assistant call tools. A payment tool is the highest-stakes tool you can add. These are the questions to ask before you do.

The risk in one sentence

An assistant that can call a payment tool will call it when its context says to. Its context includes text from emails, web pages and documents, which may have been written by someone hostile. So the safety of the payment tool cannot depend on the assistant behaving.

Seven questions to ask any payment tool

  1. What does the key allow? The assistant's key should be able to ask to pay and read payments, and nothing else. It must not create suppliers or permissions, approve payments or cancel limits.
  2. Where is the limit? In the assistant's prompt or in a service it cannot edit? Only the second counts.
  3. Is it exact? Per payment, total and speed, counted exactly.
  4. Who decides? A model judging "does this look fine" can be fooled. Signatures and numbers cannot.
  5. Can a person stop a large payment? And can the assistant approve its own? It must not be able to.
  6. What happens when something is unclear? If the bank does not answer, the tool should say "unknown" and check later, never guess, and never pay twice when the assistant retries.
  7. Is there a record? Every decision, including refusals, in a log nobody can quietly change.

What the Kaldrix connector does

Our public connector lets Claude, Cursor, GitHub Copilot in VS Code, Codex and other MCP assistants pay through Kaldrix and Zentrix, and read data through a purpose-bound permission (see database access). The assistant holds its own signing key and only signatures leave the computer. Its service key can pay and read payments and nothing else, and that is enforced by the Kaldrix service, not by the connector. A payment above your approval amount waits in your dashboard. The tools are small: who am I, add a permission, list permissions and suppliers, pay, check a payment, and query the demo database.

Questions people ask

Is it safe to let an AI assistant make payments?

Only if the limits live outside the assistant, the key can do nothing but ask to pay, and a person approves large payments.

Which MCP assistants work with Kaldrix?

Claude, Cursor, GitHub Copilot in VS Code, Codex and others that speak MCP. ChatGPT needs a hosted connector, which is not built yet.

Can the assistant create its own permissions?

No. Its key cannot create suppliers or permissions, approve payments or cancel limits. The service enforces that.

What does the assistant hold?

Its own signing key. Only signatures leave the computer.
