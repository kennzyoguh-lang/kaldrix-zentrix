Human approval for AI agent payments

Letting an agent pay is useful only if a person does not have to approve everything. The skill is choosing the few payments that need a human and making that approval mean something.

The goal: a person for the big ones, not every one

If a person approves every payment, the agent has saved nothing. If a person approves none, one trick can empty the account. The middle path is thresholds: the agent pays small things alone inside strict limits, and larger things wait.

How to set thresholds

LevelExample rule
Agent aloneUp to a small amount per payment, to listed suppliers only, inside a daily total and a speed limit.
One named personAbove the first threshold. The person sees the supplier, amount and purpose and says yes or no.
Two different peopleAbove a higher threshold. The same person cannot approve twice.
Always blockedNew suppliers not on the list, amounts above a hard ceiling, unusual hours if you want.

Five rules that make approval real

  1. Approve one exact payment. Approval of "the agent" or "this task" is too broad. The person approves this amount to this recipient for this purpose.
  2. The agent can never approve its own payment. The approval key must be different from the agent's key, and the service must refuse an approval from the agent.
  3. Show the person the facts, not the agent's summary. The agent's description can be wrong or hostile. Show the amount, recipient and purpose read from the request itself.
  4. Let a waiting payment expire. A request approved three days later may no longer be the one that was meant.
  5. Record who said yes. An auditor will ask. Keep the name, the time and the payment in a record that cannot be changed quietly.

Avoiding approval fatigue

If people get fifty requests a day they start clicking yes. Keep thresholds high enough that approvals are rare, group nothing (each approval stays one payment), and review refusals weekly so legitimate payments get a standing, narrow permission instead of a recurring human click.

Questions people ask

When should an AI agent ask a person before paying?

Above a threshold you choose, for any new supplier, or for anything outside its signed permission.

Can an agent approve its own payment?

It must never be able to. The approval key must be different from the agent's key and the service must enforce that.

What is approval fatigue?

When people get so many requests they approve without reading. Keep thresholds high and review refusals so common payments get a narrow standing permission.

Should approvals expire?

Yes. A payment that waits for days may no longer be the one that was meant.
