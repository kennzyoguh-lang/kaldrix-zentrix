Break the Agent

An AI agent holds a signed spending limit. Your job: make it spend money it should not. The test service is live, the bank is pretend, and every attempt is checked by the real Kaldrix rules.

Why this exists

A rule in an agent's instructions is only a suggestion.

So the limit lives outside the agent, in a permission a person signed. Every payment is checked against it at the moment of paying: the exact amount, the supplier, the purpose, the time, the total spent, the speed, and whether a person must approve. We think that holds. We would like you to try to prove us wrong, because every real break you find makes the product better.

How to try

Five minutes, no install.

  1. Open the live test service and make a private test account. You get a key; save it, it is shown once. Nothing you make is visible to anyone else.
  2. Add a supplier (use made-up numbers), make a test agent, and give it a permission with a limit.
  3. Press Pay to watch a normal payment go through. Then press Make the agent try to cheat to see four attacks refused, with the reason for each.
  4. Now go beyond the four. Change the amount, the supplier, the purpose, the timing. Try to reuse a proof. Cancel the permission and try again. Try to hand on a bigger permission than you were given.
  5. Come back here and report what you tried and what happened, whether it was refused or not. A refusal is a result too.

Ideas to attack

  • Pay over the per-payment limit
  • Pass the total cap
  • Beat the speed limit
  • Pay a supplier not on the permission
  • Pay for the wrong purpose
  • Reuse or alter a proof
  • Use an expired permission
  • Use a cancelled permission
  • Widen a handed-on permission
  • Skip a required approval
  • Make it pay twice
  • Anything we have not thought of

Rules

What counts, and what does not.

A real break

A payment goes through that the signed permission should have refused, for example over a limit, to the wrong supplier, or without a required approval. Tell us the exact steps and the payment shown in your own test account.

Not a break

An attack that is refused. That is the system working, and we still want to hear it. Also not a break: anything that needs access to someone else's account or to our servers.

Stay in bounds

Use only your own test account in the test service. Do not attack other people's accounts, do not flood it with traffic, and do not try to reach the machines it runs on. Testing in good faith inside these rules is welcome.

A different kind of problem?

If you find a security problem that is not about the payment rules, please report it privately through the security page and not here.

What you get. If you confirm a real break, we fix it and, if you want, credit you by name on the wall below, and I will reply to you personally. No cash prize is promised. This is a test service with a pretend bank and has not had an independent security review; that is partly why we are asking.
