The short version · two minutes

An AI agent may only do what a person signed for.

Kaldrix is a signed permission for an AI agent: which supplier it may pay and how much, or which tables it may read and why. It is checked on every request by software, not by another AI model, so a hidden instruction cannot talk it round. Zentrix moves the money, and only after checking again.

Watch it workWhat I am asking for

Pre-revenue. No customers. A working test service with a pretend bank. No real money moves.

The problem

Companies want agents to act. Nobody wants to hand over the keys.

An agent that pays suppliers, issues refunds or reads company records is useful. Today you let it only suggest, or you give it a key or a card that can do everything. One tricked agent, or one stolen key, and the account is empty or the payroll is read.

1

A person signs

What the agent may do: this supplier, this much, until this time. Or these tables, these columns, for this purpose, for this long.

2

Software checks, every time

Each request must match the signed permission exactly. No AI model decides, so nothing the agent reads can change the answer. Anything outside it is refused with the reason.

3

Money waits for proof

Zentrix checks the signature again, never pays twice, asks a person for large payments, and can hold money until the agent proves the work was done.
